Your Time Can Change a Life

Join the Annai Foundation family. Dedicate your skills, energy, and heart to create a lasting, meaningful impact on the ground.

Healthcare Support

Assist doctors in coordinating rural medical diagnostics camps, registering patients, or managing logistics.

Education Support

Teach basic subjects, run digital literacy sessions, or host storytelling activities at our rural learning shelters.

Event Volunteering

Help run our awareness rallies, fundraisers, cultural performances, and local community gatherings.

Fundraising

Assist in drafting grant proposals, connecting with local corporate CSR departments, or coordinating local collection drives.

Community Outreach

Conduct door-to-door welfare surveys, package dry food rations, or distribute educational books in remote villages.

Join as a Volunteer

Thank you for your willingness to support the Annai Foundation. Please complete the form with your details, and our coordinator will reach out to you within 3 business days to align on opportunities.

Whether you can spare a few hours a week online, or want to spend full weekends in rural villages, we have a place for your talents.

Volunteer Safety & Code

All our volunteers receive initial briefing, work alongside experienced field organizers, and are provided with local transport support for remote village duties.
