Annai Annual Charity Run & Community Fundraiser

A 5K walk and run to spread awareness about child malnutrition and raise funds to secure school nutrition items.

Digital Literacy & Coding Basics Workshop

A two-day introductory course teaching high school students standard office computing, internet applications, and basic coding constructs.

Rural Health & General Diagnostic Camp

Providing free consultations, diabetic screenings, general diagnostics, and free prescription medications to village seniors.

Distributing educational kits containing textbooks, notebooks, writing materials, and bags to over 300 primary school children.

Livelihood Training Sewing Machine Gift Event

Celebrating the graduation of our tailoring course. Distributing free industrial sewing machines and startup kits to 25 women graduates.
